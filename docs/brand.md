# AniMoku logo

The symbol is the kanji **目** (*moku*: "eye", and the first character of 目録 *mokuroku*, "catalogue"). It is drawn as an index list, with one entry sticking out like a bookmark tab: a catalogue of anime with the right entry found. The dot of the **i** in the wordmark repeats the tab's colour.

## Typography

The wordmark is set in [Outfit](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Outfit) Bold (SIL Open Font License) with −20 tracking and converted to outlines. The subtitle uses Outfit Regular.

## Usage

- Keep clear space around the logo at least equal to the height of the tab.
- Minimum size: 16 px for the symbol, 96 px wide for the horizontal logo.
- Don't recolour the tab separately from the i-dot, stretch the logo, add effects, or set "AniMoku" in another typeface next to the symbol.
- Spell the name **AniMoku**, with a capital M. Lowercase `animoku` is used only for technical identifiers such as the package, image and container names.
